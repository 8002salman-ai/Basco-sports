## Required per-SKU record

Before approval, expand each row with: manufacturer name/address/email, model number, GTIN, country of origin, fiber/materials/care data, footwear material labels where applicable, age grade, warnings/instructions, conformity/PPE evidence, image license, supplier compliance documents, market availability, and certification verification URL/date.

## Conservative gate

A product cannot become purchasable in a jurisdiction unless its required fields and documentary evidence are reviewed and its status is explicitly changed to `APPROVED_FOR_MARKET` by the responsible business owner.
