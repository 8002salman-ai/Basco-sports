# IP and product claims audit

**Checked:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** open; not legal advice.

## Required disposition

The catalog contains third-party marks and performance/certification claims that have no documentary proof in this repository. They must be removed from consumer-facing listings or supported by genuine supplier/manufacturer authorization before the relevant SKU is offered.

## Claims requiring proof or removal

- FIFA / FIFA Quality Pro — requires exact product identity, manufacturer, authorization and current FIFA verification.
- Flyknit, ACC, ZoomX, Cushlon, Dri-FIT ADV — third-party technology marks; require genuine authorized product evidence and lawful product imagery/copy.
- GORE-TEX / GTX, Vibram, Polartec — require genuine supplier/manufacturer evidence and permission to describe the product.
- Authentic, match/pro/player-spec, athlete-tested, verified athlete reviews, performance guarantees — require substantiation and authentic underlying evidence.
- Portugal origin and recycled-content claims — require actual supplier/origin and fiber documentation.
- Warranty and blister-free/technical performance guarantees — require enforceable terms and operational support.

## Image provenance

Product and category imagery currently uses Unsplash URLs and is not proven to depict the exact purchasable SKU. No product may launch until each SKU has either supplier/manufacturer imagery with commercial permission or Basco-owned photography, with provenance recorded internally (`imageSource`, `imageLicense`, `supplierImageURL`, `licenseVerifiedAt`).

## Affiliation

No affiliation with FIFA, UEFA, clubs, leagues, federations or athletes may be implied without written authorization. A disclaimer does not cure an unauthorized use.

## Required owner/supplier evidence

For each affected SKU: invoice/supplier identity, authorization to sell and use marks/images, exact model/GTIN, origin, materials, safety/conformity documents, and current certification verification source/date.
